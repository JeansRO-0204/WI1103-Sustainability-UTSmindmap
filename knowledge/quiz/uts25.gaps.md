# UTS 2025 Versi A (`uts25`): gaps and uncertainties

Source: "UTS Pengantar Prinsip Keberlanjutan 2025 Versi A" (CC BY-NC 4.0, @shiromewo). 36 questions (printed 1-7, 18-46; 8-17 do not exist). There is no answer key, so every key is `inferred` from the slides.

## (a) Premise or numbers disagree with the slides
- **u25-04**: says 6 of 9 planetary boundaries are transgressed. The course slide (2025 update) says 7 of 9 (ocean acidification newly crossed) [M2 s7, M3 s3]. The key (ozone depletion) is the same either way.
- **u25-06** (disputed): "Earths needed" is computed as footprint / Indonesia's own biocapacity (1.8/1.2 = 1.5). The Global Footprint Network convention divides by world average biocapacity (~1.6 gha), which gives ~1.1 (closest option 1.0). Indonesia is not on the slide's "How many Earths" chart [M3 s41]. Key 1.5, low confidence.
- **u25-33**: the RE labels in the options are muddled. The slide writes "Rebound RE = 1, Backfire (overconsumption) RE < 1" [M3 s22], while the literature defines backfire as RE > 1 (over 100%). Key (c) "excessive consumption (RE < 1)" is chosen by definition and matches the slide's notation.
- **u25-03**: "Maine, UK" copies the slide's error (Maine is in the USA) [M2 s5].
- **u25-07**: option (c) calls 8.09 vs 4.74 t "similar". Options (a) and (b) are both defensible; key (b).
- **u25-28**: the stem lists the responsibility dimensions as "avoid harm, do good, governance", but the slide has "long-term trade-offs (double effects)" as the third [M7 s19].
- **u25-24**: option (b) copies the slide's odd wording "engines that can increase fuel consumption" [M7 s27].
- **u25-32**: the population and per-capita CO2 figures (US 288 M, 23.92 t, etc.) are not on the slides (they look like old data).

## (b) Not covered by the slides
- **u25-41** (Goldstein fridge size/energy chart), **u25-42** (Alter & Semke: sufficiency), **u25-43** (Semke: the politics of Jevons), **u25-44** (Stuart: conspicuous consumption), **u25-45** (upfront carbon / bio-based materials), **u25-46** ("Electrify Everything Gang" vs sufficiency). These come from external articles on the Jevons Paradox and sufficiency. Only the paradox itself is in the slides [M3 s22]. Keys come from the stems' own framing and the figure, confidence medium.
- **u25-21**: the term "blue carbon" is not defined in the slides. That mangroves are blue-carbon ecosystems is general knowledge.
- **u25-39**: the slide lists Semarang's measures [M6 s24] but not the grey + green rationale. The key relies on the NbS definition [M5 s21].

## (c) Other uncertainty
- **u25-20**: the slide lists four "high potential" AFOLU options without ranking them [M6 s38]. Key (c), afforestation/reforestation/restoration, rests on list order and the IPCC figure the slide cites.
- **u25-25**: the Boar et al. synergy diagram has no explicit 7-13 arrow [M7 s18]. Key (b) rests on the s17 text (renewable energy reduces environmental impact) and on (b) being the only environmental pair.
- **u25-28**: the stem mixes "long-term impacts" (anticipation) with "stakeholder involvement" (inclusion). Key: inclusion.
